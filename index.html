Welcome to PetalVerse Library 🌺

PetalVerse Library is a digital educational platform dedicated to rare tropical flora of Indonesia.

Explore botanical knowledge and deepen your awareness of conservation and biodiversity.

  • Amorphophallus titanum
  • Rafflesia arnoldii
  • Coelogyne pandurata (Anggrek Hitam)
  • Nepenthes spp. (Kantong Semar Indonesia)

Educational Services

  • Botanical Information Resources
  • Conservation Awareness Materials
  • Flora Documentation

About PetalVerse Library

PetalVerse Library focuses on documenting and educating the public about rare tropical flora native to Indonesia.

Our mission is to promote environmental awareness and biodiversity conservation through digital literacy.
